骨科手术机器人辅助技术在创伤骨科的临床应用特征分析

李敏娟1,2           张         腾1   贾      曌3        韩    巍1       王军强1           公茂琪1∗            蒋协远1,2
(1. 首都医科大学附属北京积水潭医院创伤骨科,北京 100035;2. 北京市创伤骨科研究所,北京 100035;3. 骨科手术机器人北京
市工程研究中心,北京 100035)

【 摘要】      目的      基于真实世界数据描述手术机器人辅助技术在创伤骨科中的临床应用特征。 方法                                                          依托骨科机器人手术数据
平台,纳入 2021 年 1 月至 2023 年 7 月在首都医科大学附属北京积水潭医院创伤骨科接受手术的住院患者,收集患者的基本信
息、流行病学数据、临床诊断及手术治疗等资料,比较机器人辅助手术与非机器人辅助手术组间患者的性别、年龄、手术部位、手
术方式、手术时间和出血量。 结果                        骨科手术机器人组和非骨科手术机器人组研究对象间性别、年龄间差异均有统计学意义( P
<0. 001) 。 机器人辅助手术已应用于 14 个部位的骨折手术中。 接受机器人辅助手术的创伤骨科患者,以男性为主( 60. 61%) ,50
岁及以上患者占 39. 96%,其中 88. 07%的患者接受闭合复位内固定术[ 手术部位前三位:股骨( 35. 48%) 、骨盆( 26. 67%) 和锁骨
(20. 86%) ] 。 结论           骨科手术机器人辅助技术已广泛应用在创伤骨科多部位骨折手术中,但不同部位间该技术的具体应用仍存
在明显差异。

Analysis of clinical characteristics of robot-assisted surgery in traumatic orthopedics
Li Minjuan 1,2 , Zhang Teng 1 , Jia Zhao 3 ,Han Wei 1 , Wang Junqiang 1 , Gong Maoqi 1∗ , Jiang Xieyuan 1,2
( 1. Department of Orthopedic Trauma, Beijing Jishuitan Hospital, Capital Medical University, Beijing 100035, China;2. Beijing Research
 Institute of Traumatology and Orthopaedics, Beijing 100035, China;3. Beijing Engineering Research Center of Orthopedic Robot,Beijing
 100035, China)

【 Abstract】 Objective To describe the clinical application characteristics of robot-assisted technology in traumatic orthopedics based
on real world data. Methods Based on the orthopedic surgical robot database, this paper retrospectively included the basic information,
epidemiological data, clinical diagnosis and surgical treatment data of inpatients undergoing surgery in the orthopedic Department of
orthopedic trauma in Beijing Jishuitan Hospital Affiliated to Capital Medical University from January 2021 to July 2023. Gender, age,
surgical site, surgical type, surgical time and blood loss were compared between robot-assisted surgery and non-robot-assisted surgery
groups. Results There were significant differences in gender, age between the robot-assisted surgery group and the non-robot-assisted
surgery group ( P<0. 001) . Robot-assisted surgery technology has been used in 14 sites of fracture surgery. The majority of participants
receiving robot-assisted surgery were male ( 60. 61%) , and patients aged 50 years and above accounted for 39. 96%, among which
88. 07% of patients received closed reduction internal fixation [ the top three surgical sites: femur ( 35. 48%) , pelvis ( 26. 67%) and
clavicle (20. 86%) ] . Conclusions Robot- assisted surgery has been widely used in traumatic orthopaedic multi-site fracture surgery,
but the specific application of this technology is still different among different sites.
【 Key words】 orthopedic surgery; robot-assisted; trauma orthopedics; clinical features; real-world data; efficacy comparison

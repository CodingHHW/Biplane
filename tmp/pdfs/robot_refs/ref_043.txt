   This paper extends the previous work in three ways. First,
a cadaver study was conducted to evaluate the algorithm on
real X-rays. Second, a novel pose-dependent weighting scheme
to improve the Perspective-n-Point (PnP) solver using the
confidence of the trained network in detecting the landmarks
is introduced and third a new augmentation scheme is used to
improve the neural network’s transfer from simulated to real
data. Experiments are carried out on pelvis anatomy in the
context of trauma surgery.
